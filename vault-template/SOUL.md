# SOUL

This file is human-signed. Agents never edit it once signed; they propose changes via a note in `episodic/`.

> FILL: 200 to 500 words, drafted by your agent from your interview answers and mined evidence, then corrected and signed by you. Judgment, not rules. Cover:
> - What everything is for (the terminal value behind the work)
> - How you decide when correctness, speed, cost and polish collide
> - Your red lines, and what counts as a grey area that must stop and come to you
> - Temperament: how you talk, how direct you are, what you cannot stand
> - What growth means to you
> - The scar tissue you never want an agent to repeat
> Flaws and contradictions belong here too. A flattering soul is a useless one.
